CDC Public Health Data

County-level mortality statistics are loaded from the downloaded CDC_DATA.csv file, based on the CDC Mapping Injury, Overdose, and Violence — County dataset. County boundaries are requested from the U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb.

This dataset provides county-level mortality statistics. It does not provide individual incident coordinates, hospital-level outcomes, or a complete count of nonfatal firearm injuries in Davidson County. CDC county totals are not combined with incident reports.

Measures available in the map

  • Firearm deaths (FA_Deaths)
  • Firearm homicides (FA_Homicide)
  • Firearm suicides (FA_Suicide)

Choose an annual period or the latest trailing-12-month period in the map controls. The selected row's reporting dates and data-as-of date are shown in the county popup when provided by the CSV.

Methodology and Ethics

Currently connected source: CDC county-level mortality CSV supplied with this project. Davidson County is identified by GEOID 47037.

Geographic boundary: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb county boundary service. The Explore Map joins CDC county records to all 95 Tennessee county polygons using five-digit county GEOID/FIPS codes.

Incident-level data: No incident-level source is currently connected. No incident locations, fatal/nonfatal counts, demographic summaries, or example news reports are invented or displayed.

Map interpretation: County polygons are shaded by the CDC death rate per 100,000 residents, not by individual incident locations. Quantile classes are recalculated for the selected measure and period. Davidson County is outlined in gold. Counties without a usable rate are shown in gray.

Interpretation: County mortality statistics are based on county of residence and cannot be interpreted as hospital-level outcomes or a complete record of nonfatal injuries. Counts of 1–9 remain suppressed. CDC uses Bayesian modeled rates for areas with 1–9 deaths; the popup identifies modeled rates and shows a credible interval when supplied.

Reference resources: CDC county dataset · CDC WONDER · CDC WISQARS · MapLibre GL JS · Chart.js.

The CDC county CSV is loaded locally from Data/CDC_DATA.csv. Requires internet for the map background, Census TIGERweb county boundaries, and chart libraries. No database or live incident feed is connected.
